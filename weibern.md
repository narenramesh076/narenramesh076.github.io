# The Weierstrass approximation theorem and Bernstein polynomials (from the course "mathematical probability theory" taught by prof. Fardin Syed)

## Context and motivation

Prior to taking this course, my only exposure to probability was from statistics courses, meaning that I only saw probability as a way of analyzing data. Taking this course showed me that probability has uses in several other mathematical fields that I would have never anticipated. One of the most shocking to me was analysis.
